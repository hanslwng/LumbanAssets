📝 Troubleshooting Steps

  1. Clear Browser Cache: Press Ctrl+Shift+Delete, clear cache
  2. Hard Refresh: Press Ctrl+Shift+R or Ctrl+F5
  3. Try Different Browser: Test in Chrome, Firefox, or Edge
  4. Check Console: Press F12, look for errors in Console tab
  5. Disable Extensions: Try in Incognito/Private mode
